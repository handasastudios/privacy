The Infinite Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Infinite Quest is a math practice game for Android and iPhone (iOS), made by Handasa Studios. This policy explains how the app handles information. The short version: The Infinite Quest does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, experience points and streaks) and your settings, including the daily reminder time you choose, are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes all of it, and Settings, then Reset progress, clears your stars, experience points and streaks.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, the app asks your permission and schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose in Settings. No data is sent anywhere to do this. You can change the time or turn the reminder off in the app's Settings, or turn notifications off in your device settings, at any time.

Children

The Infinite Quest is designed for learners of all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no ads, no links to external websites, no chat, and no purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it and the date above.
